## Changes and additions in GraphStructQA

GraphStructQA selects and combines source records in a common resource
and associates them with knowledge graph structural features, SPARQL
query features, LLM responses, and answer-correctness evaluations.

These changes and additions were made by the GraphStructQA contributors.
The original dataset authors do not endorse GraphStructQA.
